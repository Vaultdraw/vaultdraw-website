## Page mode

A drawing can be an infinite canvas or simulated paper: pages of a fixed width that you scroll through vertically, like a notes app.

- **Separate pages** are sheets stacked with a gap; drawing near the end of the last one adds another. **Continuous** is one long page that grows as you fill it.
- **Sizes:** A3, A4, A5, B5, Letter, Legal, iPad 11 and 13 inch, phone, or custom; portrait or landscape.
- Nothing can be drawn or dragged outside the pages, and with separate pages a shape stays on its page. PageUp and PageDown jump between pages.
- **Paper themes:** clean white, *old school* (cream paper, blue lines, a red margin), or matching the app theme.

Set defaults for new drawings in **Settings → Page**; set one drawing from the **Page** row of the canvas menu. The setup is saved in the drawing, and other Excalidraw apps open it as a normal canvas.

## Paper

Any drawing can sit on paper: plain, grid, ruled lines with an optional margin, dots, graph paper or isometric dots. The pattern is saved with the drawing and zooms with it. Choose **Paper** in the canvas menu, or set the default in **Settings → Appearance**.

## Import a PDF

**Import: PDF…** in the palette, **Import PDF…** in the canvas menu, or dropping a `.pdf` on the canvas turns pages into images you can draw on. Pick a range such as `1-5, 8`. Pages are locked so annotating doesn't move them, and in page mode each PDF page lands on its own drawing page.

Pages are rendered by macOS, offline, and stored as ordinary images in the drawing, so it still opens on excalidraw.com. The PDF itself isn't copied into the vault.

## Export

- **Shift+Cmd+E** opens the export dialog: PNG or SVG at 1×, 2× or 3×, with or without background, dark mode, the selection only, and the scene embedded.
- **Copy PNG** or **SVG** to the clipboard from the dialog or the right-click menu.
- **Export: PDF** writes one PDF page per page in page mode.

Exports always use the drawing's own colours, even with the canvas synced to a theme.

## Mermaid

**Insert Mermaid** in the main menu or More tools turns a Mermaid diagram into editable shapes. The conversion runs offline, with no AI.
