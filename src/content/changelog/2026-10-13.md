title: Themes and a config folder
date: 2026-10-13
---

- **A config folder** like kitty's or Ghostty's: `config.toml` plus `themes/`, kept in sync with Settings and reloaded live.
- **Themes** in the Noctalia format, each with a light and a dark variant. Colours are adjusted where needed so text stays readable, tested against 200 random palettes.
- **120 Hz canvas** on ProMotion Macs.
- A quieter window: overlay title bar, compact header, a row menu in the tree.
