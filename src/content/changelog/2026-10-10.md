The first version that does the job: pick a folder, see it as a tree beside the real Excalidraw editor, and create, rename, move and delete drawings. Autosave with atomic writes, crash recovery, a dialog when something else changes a file, and the six most recent drawings on the vault's home screen. Checked in the packaged macOS app.
