title: Drag and drop, and settings per vault
date: 2026-10-14
---

- **Drag and drop in the file tree**: drawings and folders onto folders or the root, with hover-to-expand and auto-scroll.
- **Settings:** accent colour, whole-app zoom, navigator sort order, confirm before delete, and a default folder for new drawings.
- Some settings can now be stored in the vault itself, in `.excalidraw-vault/settings.toml`.
- Fixed: edits made right after moving the open drawing could be lost.
