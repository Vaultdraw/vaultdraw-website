---
title: Tabs that feel instant
date: 2026-10-21
---

- **Tabs grew up.** A tab menu, drag to reorder, pinned tabs, a list of open tabs, links that open in a new tab, and back and forward per tab.
- **Switching tabs no longer flashes.** One editor swaps drawings in place, with fonts and images loaded first, so there is never a blank frame.
- The activity bar can be reordered and its buttons hidden.
- Thumbnails are drawn in small slices, so a big folder no longer stalls the gallery.
